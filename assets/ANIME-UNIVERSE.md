## Animation and interaction
`anime-universe.js` drives the distant sky, ringed world, and near horizon at different depths using a shared scene clock. Gentle continuous drift, clamped pointer parallax and scroll displacement move the painting. The canvas adds twinkling points, tapered falling stars, up to four requested shooting stars, and a non-stacking warp. The observatory contains five camera locations, focus containment, Escape close and restored focus. Depth preference is stored locally. Motion OFF, reduced motion and hidden tabs stop decorative motion; resuming does not accumulate hidden time. Artwork load failure falls back to the full master painting. It remains visible without JavaScript.

## Live animation upgrade
The current animation upgrade adds no new generated bitmap assets. `anime-dynamics.js` reads the three existing plates and renders transparent Canvas2D overlays: latitude winds and a rotating storm on the right-hand world, cloud flow and aurora ribbons over the lower world, and a rotating vortex in the distant galaxy. Ring glints orbit in the same tilted plane as the painted rings and are occluded by the planet. Original full-resolution images remain underneath the overlays.

The scene now offers three weather presets, four surface speeds, three meteor-activity settings, slow tumbling meteors, a bounded meteor shower, a timed aurora wave, and save/recall of a favourite sky including its camera location. The observatory adds a fifth location over the lower cloud sea. Both sets of weather controls stay synchronized. The same clock drives all animation; a separate derived surface time lets speed changes remain continuous. Frame caches are bounded to three artwork types, with desktop/mobile flow updates capped at 15/12 FPS regardless of speed. Motion OFF freezes actual rendered surface pixels, and paused/hidden time is not accumulated. The drawing loops do not call the network.

Native renderer proofs and timings are generated by `tools/RenderAnimeDynamics.mjs` under the parent project's `build/anime-universe/live-surfaces`; these are artwork-rendering proofs, not browser screenshots. This upgrade changes only the website; release JAR/ZIP hashes and Minecraft artwork are preserved.
